FAQ

Questions, answered

 

Do I need a developer to install it?
No. Paste one script tag into your site, or connect WhatsApp/Messenger from the dashboard. Most stores are live in under 10 minutes.
How does it learn about my business?
Enter your website URL and it crawls your pages automatically. You can also upload documents or add FAQs manually in the Knowledge tab.
Can it really cancel and refund orders?
Yes — with guardrails. It always asks the customer to confirm first, refunds above your auto-approve limit need approval, and every action is logged in the audit trail.
What happens when the bot can't answer?
It captures the visitor's details and hands the conversation to your team with full context — no dead ends.
Which languages does it speak?
English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi and Arabic, auto-detected per visitor.
